What it is

Premium is an optional set of additional practice tools - importing your own audio, trimming a section, looping it, and related tempo tools - built on top of the free timer, not a replacement for it. It has no effect on the timer, the cooldown, or any other feature described on the home page, all of which remain free and unrestricted for every user.

Billing

Premium is offered as a monthly subscription, and separately as a one-time purchase for a permanent unlock. Payment is processed by Stripe; the price shown at checkout is the price you pay, with no hidden charges added afterwards.

Cancelling

You can cancel a Premium subscription at any time. Cancelling stops future billing - it does not end Premium immediately. Premium stays active for the rest of the period you've already paid for, and simply doesn't renew after that.

Refunds

Payments for Premium are non-refundable. This applies to a subscription period already billed and to the one-time unlock alike. The only exception is where the law that applies to you grants you a right this policy cannot remove - for example, a statutory cancellation or withdrawal right for consumers in some countries. Nothing here takes away a right you have by law; it simply means no refunds are offered beyond it.

The one network check

Outside of Premium, the app makes no network connections of any kind - see the Privacy Policy for the full statement. Premium is the single, deliberate exception: to confirm an active subscription or unlock, the app checks a small, signed license record tied to your purchase. That check does not send your name, your practice activity, or anything about how you use the app - only enough to confirm the license is valid. It happens only for people who have chosen to use Premium; the free timer never makes this or any other network call.
